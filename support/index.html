Fino support

Fino for iPhone

Fino turns a bag of coffee into a first recipe for your grinder and brewer, then suggests one change at a time until the cup tastes right.

Questions, a wrong number, a grinder that’s missing: write to mario@mariocanas.com. Say which grinder and brewer you use and what you expected to see. A screenshot helps.

Which ways of brewing does Fino support?

Espresso, pour over (V60), Chemex, AeroPress, French press, moka pot, cold brew and bean-to-cup machines.

My grinder isn’t in the list

When you set up your gear, tap “My grinder isn’t listed…” and describe its dial. Your first brews set it right.

Where does the first grind come from?

From the bag and your setup: the roast, the dose and your grinder’s range. Tap “Why?” to see each step. It’s an informed first guess. After each brew, tell Fino how it went and it suggests the next move, learning how your grinder really behaves.

Adding a coffee

Tap “Add coffee”, then take a photo of the bag or paste the link to the coffee’s page in the shop. In Safari, Share › Fino does the same. If the bag or page doesn’t state the roast, Fino asks for it. Check the details on the coffee’s page, where you can correct anything. What Fino can’t read stays empty rather than guessed.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Where it’s available, Fino uses it to read unusual wording on labels and in your notes. Everything else works the same without it.

Widgets and Siri

Add Fino’s widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen to see the grind for your coffees. Or ask Siri, “What’s my grind in Fino?”

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
